City sewer in town, septic around it

The City of Newton runs its own water and wastewater utilities, so inside the city most homes are on sewer. Around Newton, toward Claremont, Startown, Catawba and the county roads, septic systems are common, and so are older houses: Newton's median year built is 1977, older than Hickory, Conover or Maiden, with about 28% of homes built before 1960. Confirm how a particular house handles wastewater before you order; the tax record, the seller's disclosure statement and a look for a tank lid or cleanout in the yard usually settle it.

Getting Catawba County septic records

Catawba County Environmental Health, part of Catawba County Public Health, permits septic systems and private wells through its On-Site Water Protection program. The county government offices are at 25 Government Drive in Newton (828-465-8200), and copies of existing septic permits can be requested through an online form on the county's Environmental Health forms page; the division's email is EHAdmin@catawbacountync.gov. Newer applications run through the county's ePermit system.

Ask for three things: the permit, because its bedroom count is the first comparison the state's inspection rule calls for; the layout drawing, because it puts the tank where it belongs; and the soil evaluation, which NC State Extension notes is also kept by the local health department. If the house has gained bedrooms since the permit was issued, that's the conversation to have early. Records guide.

What the inspection covers

Every inspection follows the state's minimum point-of-sale standard (21 NCAC 39 .1006) and the same order: compare the advertised bedroom count with the permit, locate and mark the tank, uncover and open it, evaluate the tank and its components, evaluate the pump tank and control panel if there is one, check the drain field area, and put it in writing with photos. If you want the tank pumped, I'll arrange a pumping company for the same visit. The full process, with photos of each step, is on the septic inspection page.

Newton septic FAQ

How do I request a Catawba County septic permit?

Through the online permit-copy request on the county's Environmental Health forms page, or by emailing EHAdmin@catawbacountync.gov. Include the property address and parcel number.

The house has more bedrooms than the permit. What happens?

The report notes the difference, because the state's inspection rule starts with that comparison. What happens next is between the buyer, the seller and the county: a system permitted for three bedrooms is designed for three bedrooms of wastewater, and the county's environmental health office is the authority on what it would take to change that.
