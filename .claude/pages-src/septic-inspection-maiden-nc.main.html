Septic Inspections in Maiden, NC

Maiden straddles the Catawba-Lincoln county line, and the line decides which office has the septic permit. I inspect septic systems on both sides, from in-town lots to the rural properties along US 321 and toward Lake Norman's upper reaches.

Two counties, one town

Inside town limits, Maiden buys water from the City of Hickory and runs its own sewer system. Outside, septic is common, and especially so on the Lincoln County side: an older NCDHHS table put about four in five Lincoln County homes in the Catawba River basin on septic, a noticeably higher share than the same table showed for Catawba County. Maiden's median year built is 1984, with about one in five homes built before 1960 and about 8% mobile or manufactured homes, so the systems range from older tanks with no record to 2000s subdivisions with everything on file.

Which office has the permit

For a parcel on the Catawba side, the records are with Catawba County Environmental Health in Newton, which takes online requests for a copy of an existing permit (EHAdmin@catawbacountync.gov, county line 828-465-8200). On the Lincoln side, Lincoln County Environmental Health (Lincoln County Health Department, 353 N. Generals Blvd., Lincolnton) has a "Request Septic or Well Permit" option on its page; include the property address or parcel number. The county on the tax record is the one to call. Records guide.

Wells and septic together

Many Maiden-area properties on septic are on a private well too, and the two systems are neighbors underground. The state's inspection records the type of water supply, and if the well is private, a water test through Inspect-All Home Inspections can be added to the same visit. EPA counts elevated nitrate or coliform in a nearby well among the signs of a septic problem, which is one more reason to look at both.

What the inspection covers

Every inspection follows the state's minimum point-of-sale standard (21 NCAC 39 .1006) and the same order: compare the advertised bedroom count with the permit, locate and mark the tank, uncover and open it, evaluate the tank and its components, evaluate the pump tank and control panel if there is one, check the drain field area, and put it in writing with photos. If you want the tank pumped, I'll arrange a pumping company for the same visit. The full process, with photos of each step, is on the septic inspection page.

Maiden septic FAQ

How do I know which county my Maiden property is in?

The county tax record for the parcel. Catawba County's office is in Newton; Lincoln County's is in Lincolnton. The septic permit is with whichever county the parcel sits in.

Can you inspect the septic and test the well on the same visit?

Yes. The septic inspection is this site's service; well water testing is added through Inspect-All Home Inspections, and both happen in one trip.

Do you cover the Lincoln County side, toward Lincolnton and Denver?

Yes. The Maiden area on both sides of the line is part of my service area, and I'll go further into Lincoln County on request.
