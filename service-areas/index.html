Why the county matters

The inspection is the same everywhere: it follows the state's minimum point-of-sale standard (21 NCAC 39 .1006) in every county. What changes from county to county is the paperwork. Each one keeps its own septic permits, in its own system, going back a different number of years. Buncombe didn't issue septic permits before 1977. Burke has a request form built for agents. McDowell's records live in a district portal. Catawba takes permit-copy requests online. Alexander has no online search at all. The county records guide walks through all of them.

How far I travel

Morganton is home base, and the eleven towns above are a regular week. The lakes, the river valleys and the mountain communities between them are all in range; if a property is within an hour or so of Morganton, assume yes and call. Beyond that, ask. A pumping company can usually be arranged anywhere in the area for the same visit.
